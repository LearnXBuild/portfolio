Things I believe
01

Simplicity is not the absence of effort. It is the result of it. The cleanest systems take the most rework.

02

Build in public. Showing the work, including the failed attempts, is more valuable than polished outcomes.

03

Speed and reliability are not tradeoffs. Most systems that are slow are also unreliable. Fix the foundations.

04

Photography and engineering share the same core skill: seeing what's actually there, not what you expect to be.
